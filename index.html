About Dustyn

A Leader Ready to Serve

20 Years of Professional Excellence

Dustyn Dorn brings nearly two decades of experience as an IT professional in financial services to his campaign for District 60. His expertise spans market data, Critical Incident Management, IT Service Management, and vendor management.

As a Democrat running for the PA House, Dustyn is committed to working across party lines to pass legislation. His goal is clear: increase trust in government while improving the quality of life for all Pennsylvanians.

As a dedicated family man and community member, Dustyn understands the challenges facing Pennsylvania families and is ready to bring his problem-solving skills to Harrisburg.
